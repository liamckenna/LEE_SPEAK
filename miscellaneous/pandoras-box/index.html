Pandora's Box Has Been Opened

There is no going back. The world will probably end. If it doesn’t, it will look nothing like anything we know now. There is nothing any of us can do.

There is no hope. Only fear and fixation. I cannot look away. It only hurts to watch but what else can I do? Choose to remain in ignorance, lay down, and truly accept the fate of the world? Even if that’s the right answer, how could I bring myself to do so?

Why does everyone act like everything is fine? It’s brushed off with jokes and dismissals. I think we all know, subconsciously or otherwise. Maybe some of us have an easy time turning a blind eye. I am envious. I cannot help but spread this fear like a virus. I am sorry.

Truly, what else am I supposed to make of this?
